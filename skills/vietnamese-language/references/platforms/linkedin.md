## Register and Honorifics

- Full name (Nguyễn Văn A) is used on profiles
- Superiors and executives are referred to with title, not first name alone
- Default address: anh/chị
- Industry jargon commonly kept in English (ROAS, SaaS, B2B)

## Job Posting Structure

- Company intro → Role → Requirements → Benefits → Application method
