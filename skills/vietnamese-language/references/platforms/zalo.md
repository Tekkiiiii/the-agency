# Zalo OA — Platform Notes

> For Zalo broadcast STRATEGY and KPIs → marketing/14-email-marketing/.

## Mechanics

- ZNS messages: 100–200 characters body before link (unverified; check current Zalo ZNS docs before relying on it)
- Personalization fields: name, birthday, order number
- OA: brand account with structured messages, broadcast and customer service; Personal: individual account

## Register

- Customer service: customer addressed as Anh/Chị, never bạn; sentence-final "ạ" marks respectful register
- Self-reference in CS: brand name, not tôi/mình
- Zalo is commonly used for boss/employee and parent/child communication, so register is formal-polite
