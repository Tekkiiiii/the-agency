## Age-Segment Register Differences

- 18–25: peer register (bạn/mình), English loanwords common
- 26–35: bạn/mình register, more formal CTAs
- 36–50: formal register
- 50+: full formal (anh/chị/quý vị), no loanwords

## Marketplace Conventions

- Location convention: district + city
- Reply register: match the commenter's address term (chị → chị)
