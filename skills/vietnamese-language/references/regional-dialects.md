## Tone System Differences

### Northern (Hà Nội standard — 6 tones)
- Sắc (´), huyền (`), hỏi (?), ngã (~), nặng (.), ngang (no mark)
- Ngã and hỏi are distinct tones
- Standard broadcast Vietnamese is Northern-based
- This is the "textbook" standard taught in schools

### Southern (5 functional tones)
- Ngã (~) and hỏi (?) merge into one perceived tone in speech
- Written form maintains full diacritics (it's a spoken difference, not written)
- Content "sounding Southern" uses Southern vocabulary, not different diacritics

### Central (Huế, Đà Nẵng)
- 6 tones with different phonetic realizations from Northern
- Heaviest regional accent in spoken Vietnamese
- Rarely targeted in digital content except hyper-local brands

## Diaspora Vietnamese (Việt kiều)

### Key differences from mainland Vietnamese
- Vocabulary from 1975-era South Vietnam (pre-unification terms still used)
- xe hơi (diaspora/South) vs ô tô (North) for "car"
- More formal or archaic structures in some diaspora communities
- Marketing targeting Việt kiều: Southern-leaning or neutral preferred

### By host country
- US Việt kiều: English-dominant with Vietnamese code-switching
- France Việt kiều: some French loanwords remain
- Australia Việt kiều: similar to US pattern
