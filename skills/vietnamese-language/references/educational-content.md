# Educational Content — Vietnamese Conventions

> For academic connectors in formal writing → references/formal-documents.md.

## French Colonial Pedagogical Legacy

- Deductive structure preference: rule stated first, then examples (inverse of English "show then tell")
- Formal register in educational contexts signals credibility even on TikTok
- Lists and numbered steps are culturally expected in instructional content
- Tables and visual hierarchies are trusted and expected

## Ministry of Education Language Standards

- Approved educational vocabulary and standard terms (Bộ GD&ĐT norms)
- Banned casual terms in formal educational contexts (no teen code, no slang)
- Approved connective expressions: ngoài ra, bên cạnh đó, tuy nhiên, mặc dù, do đó, vì vậy, dẫn đến
- Authority signal: "Theo chương trình Bộ GD&ĐT..." (According to the Ministry curriculum...)
- Citation: footnotes preferred over in-text citations in academic Vietnamese

Voice comes from exemplars (brand/voice-exemplars-vi, pending). Do not copy phrasing from this file.

## Step-by-Step Formatting Preferences

- Numbered steps are the expected format for instruction (bullets less conventional)
- Each step: max 2 sentences for EduTok; unlimited for long-form
- Sub-steps: a/b/c or dash (—)
- Sequence cue words: đầu tiên, tiếp theo, sau đó, cuối cùng

## Deductive Structure Conventions

Vietnamese educational audiences expect:
1. Claim / conclusion stated upfront
2. Supporting reasoning
3. Example
4. Application or summary

NOT: example → reasoning → conclusion (inductive, common in Western pedagogy)

## Expert Attribution Patterns

- First reference: "Theo [Tên], [chức danh], [tổ chức]:"
- Second reference: honorific + surname
- Research: cite year and institution
- Do NOT use "studies show" without naming the study — VN audience demands specificity

## E-Learning Platform Language

### Vietnamese platforms (e.g. Kyna)
- Module naming: "Phần [N]: [Tên chủ đề]"

### Corporate e-learning (e.g. Udemy, LinkedIn Learning)
- B2B register in course descriptions: formal

## Academic Connectors Reference

| Function | Connectors |
|---|---|
| Addition | ngoài ra, bên cạnh đó, đồng thời |
| Contrast | tuy nhiên, mặc dù, nhưng, trong khi đó |
| Cause | do đó, vì vậy, bởi vì, dẫn đến |
| Sequence | đầu tiên, tiếp theo, sau đó, cuối cùng |
| Summary | tóm lại, nhìn chung, kết luận |

### Avoid in formal educational writing
- Colloquial connectors: mà, rồi, thì
- Sentence-initial conjunctions in academic (acceptable in EduTok, not in papers)
